📄 Buying guide: JK vs JKU seat covers

The JK and JKU share the same front seat frame, so most front-seat covers fit both. The key differences are:

  • 2-Door JK rear seats: fold-forward buckets — buy a 2-door specific rear set
  • 4-Door JKU rear seats: split-fold bench — buy a 4-door/JKU rear set
  • Year range: All JK years (2007-2018) use the same seat frame. Most covers fit all years.
  • Airbag compatibility: The JK has side-impact airbags in the seat. Always confirm your cover has airbag-safe seams.
  • Bartact note: Bartact makes separate covers for 2-door vs 4-door. Order the right one from their site.
