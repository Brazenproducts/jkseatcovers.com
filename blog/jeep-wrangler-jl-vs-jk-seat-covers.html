Jeep Wrangler JL vs JK Seat Covers — Do They Fit the Same?

Fitment guide · Updated 2026

Short answer: no — JK and JL Wrangler seat covers are not interchangeable. It's the single most common seat-cover buying mistake. Jeep redesigned the seats when the Wrangler moved from the JK generation to the JL, so covers cut for one won't fit the other properly. Here's what actually differs and how to buy right.

Which Is Which

  • JK / JKU: 2007–2018 Wrangler (2-door JK, 4-door JKU). Note: 2018 was a crossover year — both the last JK and first JL were sold, so verify your build.
  • JL / JLU: 2018–present Wrangler (2-door JL, 4-door JLU).

Why They Don't Interchange

  • Seat shape & bolstering: JL seats have more contoured, supportive bolsters than the flatter JK seats. A JK cover will pull and gap on a JL seat.
  • Headrests: Mounting and shape changed between generations, so headrest sleeves differ.
  • Airbags: Side-airbag placement and seams differ. Using the wrong generation's cover can misalign the airbag release seam — a safety issue.
  • Controls & cutouts: Lever, recline, and seatbelt-guide positions moved, so cover cutouts won't line up.

The 2018 Trap

Because both JK and JL were sold in 2018, "2018 Wrangler" isn't specific enough. Check your door jamb, VIN, or dash — JL interiors have the larger central touchscreen and updated climate controls. Buy covers labeled for JK or JL explicitly, matched to which one you actually have.

How to Buy the Right Set

Always select covers listed for your exact generation (JK/JKU or JL/JLU) and year, confirm airbag compatibility if your seats have side airbags, and pick your material by use — neoprene for all-around, Cordura for hard off-road. Quality brands sell JK and JL as separate SKUs for exactly this reason.

Bottom Line

Don't assume a JL cover fits a JK (or vice versa) — they're different seats. Match the generation, year, and airbag spec and you'll get a clean, safe fit. This site focuses on JK/JKU; see our top JK seat cover picks and how-to-choose guide.
