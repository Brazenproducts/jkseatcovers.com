Jeep Gladiator Seat Cover Options — JT Fitment & Material Guide

Fitment guide · Updated 2026

The Jeep Gladiator (JT) shares a lot of DNA with the JL Wrangler, but when it comes to seat covers — especially the rear — there are real differences. If you're shopping covers for a 2020+ Gladiator, here's what you need to know to buy the right set.

Gladiator Front Seats vs. Wrangler

The Gladiator's front seats are very close to the JL Wrangler's, so many front covers are shared across JL/JT fitments. Still, always confirm the listing covers the Gladiator specifically, and match your airbag configuration — most Gladiators have seat-mounted side airbags, so covers must be airbag-compatible with a release seam.

The Rear Seat Is Where It Differs

This is the key point: the Gladiator's rear bench is not the same as the Wrangler JLU. The JT rear seat is designed with under-seat lockable storage and a different fold mechanism. Rear covers must be cut for the Gladiator specifically or they'll block the storage and won't fold correctly. Don't assume a JLU rear cover fits a JT.

Material Options

  • Neoprene: Water-resistant and comfortable — the most popular all-around choice for a Gladiator that sees trail, beach, and daily use.
  • 1000D Cordura / canvas: Maximum durability for work-truck and hard off-road use; resists tools, abrasion, and UV.
  • Leatherette: A dressier look for a Gladiator used more as a daily driver — less rugged than Cordura.

MOLLE & Storage Options

Because the Gladiator is a pickup used for gear hauling, MOLLE seatback covers are especially popular — they turn the back of the front seats into mountable storage for recovery gear, tools, and tie-downs. USA-made brands like Bartact offer airbag-compatible JT covers with integrated MOLLE panels.

Bottom Line

For a Gladiator: front covers often overlap with JL Wrangler, but buy JT-specific rear covers so you keep the under-seat storage and fold function.
